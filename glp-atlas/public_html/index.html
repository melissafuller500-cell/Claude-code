What is a GLP-1?

A GLP-1 receptor agonist is a medicine that mimics the gut hormone GLP-1: it raises insulin after meals, lowers glucagon, slows stomach emptying and reduces appetite. Semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound) are the best known; all are prescription-only in the US and Canada. Read the full answer →
