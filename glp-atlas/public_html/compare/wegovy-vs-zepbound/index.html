Wegovy vs Zepbound: semaglutide vs tirzepatide for weight loss

✓Last reviewed October 20, 2026 · Written by Noé Mbwang · Medical review: pending — clinician review in progress · How we review

Quick answer

Wegovy (semaglutide) vs Zepbound (tirzepatide): weight-loss results, dosing, side effects and self-pay costs compared. In head-to-head research tirzepatide produced greater average weight loss than semaglutide 2.4 mg. Wegovy HD narrows that gap. Side effects are similar and mostly gastrointestinal.
