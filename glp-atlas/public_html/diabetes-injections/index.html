Diabetes injections — every option

✓Last reviewed October 20, 2026 · Written by Noé Mbwang · Medical review: pending — clinician review in progress · How we review

Complete list of diabetes injections in 2026: GLP-1s (Ozempic, Mounjaro, Trulicity, Victoza), insulins (Lantus, Humalog, Tresiba, Awiqli), combinations and glucagon.

In short

Weekly: Ozempic, Mounjaro, Trulicity, Bydureon BCise. Daily: Victoza / liraglutide. These lower blood sugar and often body weight, with low risk of hypoglycemia on their own.

Non-insulin injections (GLP-1 and dual agonists)

Insulins

Basal (long-acting): glargine, degludec, and the once-weekly Awiqli. Mealtime: lispro, aspart, glulisine. Human insulins: regular, NPH, premixed.

Combinations

Soliqua (glargine + lixisenatide) and Xultophy (degludec + liraglutide).

Other

Symlin (pramlintide) with mealtime insulin, and glucagon for emergencies.
