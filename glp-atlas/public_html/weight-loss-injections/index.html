Weight loss injections and pills

✓Last reviewed October 20, 2026 · Written by Dr. George Grunberger · Medical review: pending — clinician review in progress · How we review

Every FDA-approved weight loss injection and GLP-1 pill in 2026: Wegovy, Wegovy HD, Zepbound, Saxenda, Wegovy pill and Foundayo — results, doses and cost.

In short

Ozempic and Mounjaro are diabetes brands; prescribing them for weight loss is off-label. CagriSema and retatrutide are not approved.

Who qualifies?

US labels cover adults with a BMI of 30 or more, or 27 or more with a weight-related condition such as high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes or high cholesterol. Some products are also approved for adolescents.
