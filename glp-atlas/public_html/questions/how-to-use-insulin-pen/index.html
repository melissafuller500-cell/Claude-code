Question

How to use an insulin pen

✓Last reviewed October 20, 2026 · Written by Dr. George Grunberger · Medical review: pending — clinician review in progress · How we review

Short answer

Attach a new needle, prime with 2 units, dial the dose, inject at 90 degrees into the stomach or thigh, hold for about 10 seconds, then remove and discard the needle. Rotate sites. Never share pens.

General information based on official labels. Your prescriber or pharmacist can advise on your situation.
