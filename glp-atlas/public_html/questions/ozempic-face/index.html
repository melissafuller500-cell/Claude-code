Question

What is 'Ozempic face'?

✓Last reviewed October 20, 2026 · Written by Dr. George Grunberger · Medical review: pending — clinician review in progress · How we review

Short answer

A popular term for a hollow or aged facial look after rapid weight loss. It is caused by loss of facial fat, not by a direct effect of the drug, and can happen with any large weight loss.

General information based on official labels. Your prescriber or pharmacist can advise on your situation.
