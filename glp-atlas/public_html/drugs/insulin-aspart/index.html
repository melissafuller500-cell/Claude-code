Insulin aspart: NovoLog, Fiasp

insulin aspart · Rapid-acting (mealtime) insulin · Novo Nordisk

✓Last reviewed October 20, 2026 · Written by Noé Mbwang · Medical review: pending — clinician review in progress · How we review

In short

Insulin aspart (insulin aspart) is a rapid-acting (mealtime) insulin made by Novo Nordisk, used for mealtime blood sugar control in type 1 and type 2 diabetes. Schedule: with meals. It is prescription-only in the US and Canada.

FormVials, pens, cartridges
How oftenWith meals
United StatesFDA-approved; unbranded insulin aspart is available.
CanadaAvailable in Canada (NovoRapid, Fiasp).
PrescriptionRequired

What it is used for

  • Mealtime blood sugar control in type 1 and type 2 diabetes

Insulin aspart dosage

Per the official label. Your prescriber sets your dose — never change it on your own.

Insulin aspart label dosing schedule
1DoseIndividualized in units by your prescriber
2TimingNovoLog: 5–10 minutes before a meal. Fiasp: at the start of a meal or within 20 minutes after starting

How to take it

Under the skin; also used in pumps.

Storage

Unopened: refrigerate. In use: room temperature up to 28 days.

Side effects

Common

  • Low blood sugar (hypoglycemia)
  • Injection-site reactions
  • Weight gain
  • Lipodystrophy or skin thickening at repeated injection sites

Serious — get medical help

  • Severe hypoglycemia (confusion, seizures, loss of consciousness)
  • Low potassium (hypokalemia)
  • Fluid retention and heart failure when combined with thiazolidinediones
  • Serious allergic reactions

Who should not use it

  • During an episode of low blood sugar
  • Never share pens or needles, even if the needle is changed
  • Do not switch insulin type, brand or concentration without your prescriber
