In short

CagriSema (cagrilintide + semaglutide) is an investigational amylin analog + GLP-1 receptor agonist combination from Novo Nordisk. It is not approved and cannot legally be bought outside a registered clinical trial. Not FDA-approved. Filed December 18, 2025; Novo Nordisk guides to an FDA decision in Q4 2026.

Can you buy CagriSema?

No. It is not approved, so there is no legal way to purchase or order it outside a registered clinical trial. Search ClinicalTrials.gov↗ for trials near you.

Frequently asked questions

How did CagriSema compare with tirzepatide?

In REDEFINE 4 (Feb 2026), CagriSema achieved 23.0% weight loss versus 25.5% with tirzepatide 15 mg and missed its non-inferiority goal.

Can I buy CagriSema online?

No. Anything sold as CagriSema today is unapproved and unverified.
