Glucagon rescue: GlucaGen, Gvoke, Baqsimi

glucagon / dasiglucagon · Emergency treatment for severe low blood sugar · Various

✓Last reviewed October 20, 2026 · Written by Dr. George Grunberger · Medical review: pending — clinician review in progress · How we review

In short

Glucagon (glucagon / dasiglucagon) is an emergency treatment for severe low blood sugar made by Various, used for severe hypoglycemia in people with diabetes. Schedule: emergency use only. It is prescription-only in the US and Canada.

FormInjection kit, prefilled pen/syringe, nasal powder (Baqsimi)
How oftenEmergency use only
United StatesFDA-approved; several generics.
CanadaAvailable in Canada.
PrescriptionRequired

What it is used for

  • Severe hypoglycemia in people with diabetes

Glucagon dosage

Per the official label. Your prescriber sets your dose — never change it on your own.

Glucagon label dosing schedule
1UseFollow the product's emergency instructions; call emergency services after use

How to take it

Given by a family member or bystander when the person cannot safely eat or drink.

Storage

Room temperature; check expiry dates.

Emergency steps

Side effects

Common

  • Nausea
  • Diarrhea
  • Vomiting
  • Constipation
  • Abdominal pain
  • Indigestion / heartburn
  • Fatigue
  • Decreased appetite

Serious — get medical help

  • Pancreatitis (severe stomach pain that may spread to the back)
  • Gallbladder problems, including gallstones
  • Low blood sugar when combined with insulin or a sulfonylurea
  • Kidney problems, often from dehydration after vomiting or diarrhea
  • Serious allergic reactions
  • Diabetic retinopathy complications (people with type 2 diabetes)
  • Pulmonary aspiration during anesthesia or deep sedation — tell your surgical team you take it

Who should not use it

  • Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma, or MEN 2
  • Prior serious allergic reaction to the drug or its ingredients
  • Pregnancy (stop before a planned pregnancy as advised by your prescriber)
  • Use together with another GLP-1 receptor agonist

Cost

Generic and brand options; insurance usually covers rescue glucagon.

No. It is a rescue medicine for severe low blood sugar.
