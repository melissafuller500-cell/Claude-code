How to buy GLP-1 medicines legally in Canada

✓Last reviewed October 20, 2026 · Written by Noé Mbwang · Medical review: pending — clinician review in progress · How we review

How to get Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro and generic semaglutide legally in Canada: prescriptions, pharmacies, coverage and the new generics.

Key point

In Canada, GLP-1 medicines are prescription drugs dispensed by provincially licensed pharmacies.

Get a prescription

From a family doctor, nurse practitioner, or a licensed virtual-care provider in your province.

Generic semaglutide is here

Health Canada authorized the first generic semaglutide (Ozempic equivalent) on April 28, 2026 and a second on May 1, 2026, and the first generic for weight management on June 29, 2026. Ask your pharmacist whether a generic can be substituted under your plan and province.

Verify the pharmacy

Every pharmacy must be licensed by its provincial regulatory college. Look it up on your college's public register before ordering online.

Coverage

Provincial plans and private insurers set their own rules; many require prior authorization. Obesity coverage is often an optional benefit.
