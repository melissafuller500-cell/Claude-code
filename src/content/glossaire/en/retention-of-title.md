## Explanation

It mainly concerns trading and the supply of goods. It must be agreed in writing, before delivery: in the contract, in terms of sale accepted by the client, or on the signed purchase order. It does not replace tracking unpaid invoices, but it gives weight to your request for payment.

## Example

*Fictional example.* A trader delivers 2,800,000 FCFA of electrical equipment with a signed retention-of-title clause. As long as the invoice is unpaid, the equipment legally remains theirs. Indicative wording, to have reviewed: *general information, not legal advice*.
