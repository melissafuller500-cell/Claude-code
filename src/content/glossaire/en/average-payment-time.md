## Explanation

It is also called DSO, for *Days Sales Outstanding*. It is the number that sums up how you get paid: the higher it is, the longer your money sleeps at your clients’. Compare it with the terms written in your contracts: the gap between the two is your average delay.

A simple method for an SME: divide the total of unpaid invoices by the revenue invoiced over the period, then multiply by the number of days in the period.

## Example

*Fictional example.* A company invoices 30,000,000 FCFA over the last 90 days. On the last day, 20,000,000 FCFA of invoices are still unpaid. Its average payment time is 20,000,000 ÷ 30,000,000 × 90 = 60 days. If its contracts say 30 days, its clients pay on average 30 days late.

This is the number measured at the start of the engagement, then every month.
