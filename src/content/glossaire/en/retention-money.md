## Explanation

In French it is the *retenue de garantie*. Its rate and release date are set by the contract. The risk for the company is simple: forgetting it. Retention is released months after the site closes, when the team is already elsewhere. Good practice fits in one line: for each project, enter the amount retained and the release date in the tracker, and follow up on that date.

## Example

*Fictional example.* On a 40,000,000 FCFA contract with 5% retention, 2,000,000 FCFA stay with the client until final acceptance. Without a date in the tracker, that money may never be claimed.
