## Explanation

In French it is the *PV de réception*. It is the proof the work was done. Without it, the invoice is open to dispute: the client can question delivery, quantity or quality when payment falls due. With it, the claim becomes hard to challenge. For a public client, a signed acceptance report is part of the file without which the invoice does not move.

A useful clause sets a deadline for written reservations: after that, the service is deemed accepted.

## Example

*Fictional example.* A maintenance company has the client’s site manager sign a job sheet every month. Its 1,350,000 FCFA invoice comes with those sheets: the discussion is about the payment date, not about whether the service happened.
