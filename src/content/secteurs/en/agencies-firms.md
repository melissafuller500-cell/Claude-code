## Where the money gets stuck

- **Work started without a deposit.** You finance the client’s project with your own cash.
- **Endless back-and-forth on deliverables.** As long as the work is not “finished”, the final invoice does not go out.
- **The final instalment held back “until satisfied”.** A test that only the client can declare met.

## The clauses that matter

- **A deposit paid before starting**: work starts upon receipt of the deposit.
- **The number of included revisions, written in the quote**, and the price of extra revisions.
- **A payment schedule by milestone**, each milestone approved in writing, with an approval deadline: after it, the milestone is deemed accepted.
- **Payment terms in days** for each instalment.

## Worked example

*Fictional example.* An engineering firm signs a 6,000,000 FCFA assignment, payable in full on delivery of the final report. The report goes through five versions over four months. With a 30% deposit and two instalments on milestone approval, the firm would have collected **1,800,000 FCFA** at signing and **4,200,000 FCFA** along the way, instead of waiting for the end.
